# Phase 3 Directional Service-Imbalance Preregistration

## Evidence boundary

This is the final planned Q3 targeted post-hoc supplementary validation. It is
independent of, and must not replace or modify, frozen Phase 1, Phase 2, or
completed Phase 3 evidence. All analysis is descriptive for three seeds.

## Mechanism rationale

Production `cooperative_comparator.py` ranks legal candidates by group size
first, then aggregate waiting, maximum waiting, minimum ETA, and candidate ID.
Waiting therefore cannot cause a smaller candidate to outrank an available
larger candidate. The grant lifecycle persists one selected grant until
clearance or timeout, but contains no cross-epoch service memory. Candidate
generation and final safety verification are geometric/safety mechanisms and
contain no anti-starvation rule.

The formal Gemini context exposes per-vehicle waiting, approach, movement,
speed, distance and ETA, plus candidate group size, aggregate waiting, maximum
waiting, movement/approach composition and minimum ETA. A planner difference
is therefore mechanistically possible without asking Gemini to perform safety
geometry.

Historical support is `HISTORICAL_SUPPORT_PARTIAL`. Frozen S3-12V seeds 1--3
each contain one epoch where a legal opposite-STRAIGHT pair (S2) lost to an
all-RIGHT four-vehicle candidate (R4), followed by additional waiting before
S2 service. The retained records do not show repeated S2 non-service across
multiple decision epochs, so they motivate but do not establish starvation.

## Fixed scenario

- Condition: `DIRECTIONAL_SERVICE_IMBALANCE_STRESS`
- Topology/control: existing S3 network, control zone, candidate generator,
  comparator, grant lifecycle and safety verifier.
- Vehicles: 16.
- Seeds: 1, 2, 3.
- Jitter: existing S3 `0..1 s` seeded departure jitter.
- Car-following sigma: existing `0.5`.
- Duration: 300 s.
- Routes: `N_W,E_N,S_E,W_S,N_S,S_N,E_S,W_N,N_W,E_N,S_E,W_S,N_W,E_N,S_E,W_S`.
- Base departures: `0,0,1,1,3,3,5,5,9,9,10,10,18,18,19,19 s`.

The first 12 vehicles exactly retain the S3-12V demand mechanics. The only
manipulation is one additional R4 reinforcement wave at the existing 9 s wave
spacing. This creates repeated larger-compatible-group availability while the
original N/S S2 pair may wait. Waiting is generated by closed-loop SUMO, never
written into planner state. No parameter sweep is permitted.

## Operational definitions

Vehicle waiting is each vehicle's maximum recorded SUMO waiting value over the
episode. P95 uses Hyndman-Fan type 7 linear interpolation. Approach means use
those per-vehicle maxima grouped by incoming edge.

- Maximum vehicle waiting.
- P95 vehicle waiting.
- Maximum approach mean waiting.
- Approach waiting range: maximum minus minimum approach mean.
- Repeated eligible-but-not-selected count: decision epochs where the exact
  original S2 pair is legal, a larger candidate is legal, and S2 is not selected.
- Longest consecutive eligible-but-not-selected sequence.

The existing canonical decision records contain candidate sets/features,
selected candidates, provider/parser/fallback provenance, latency and grant
lifecycle. The derived observer adds the target history and episode waiting
distribution. It does not affect decisions.

## Stage 1 deterministic feasibility gate

Matrix: `DETERMINISTIC_CANDIDATE x seeds 1,2,3` (three episodes, zero Gemini
calls). A seed passes only when all conditions hold:

1. 16 departed, 16 arrived, completion rate 1.0.
2. Zero collisions, safety interventions and grant timeouts.
3. The exact S2 target is legal in at least two decision epochs.
4. It is eligible with a larger legal candidate but not selected at least twice.
5. The longest such consecutive sequence is at least two epochs.
6. Its aggregate waiting while not selected exceeds 24 s, the maximum frozen
   S3-12V epoch-3 S2 aggregate waiting across seeds 1--3.

The Stage 1 gate passes only if at least two of three seeds pass. Gemini
hypothetical choices and relative traffic performance are not gate inputs.
Gate failure permanently stops this supplementary experiment sequence.

## Stage 2 matched evaluation

Stage 2 is withheld until Stage 1 has been run locally, audited, passed, and a
new explicit human authorization is provided. It adds exactly three
`GEMINI_CANDIDATE` episodes for the same seeds and demand signatures. Stage 1
deterministic runs are reused, producing six total matched episodes.

Gemini uses `STRICT_LLM_MODE=true`. A valid Gemini episode requires at least
one valid LLM decision, zero failed decisions, zero fallback decisions and
`llm_episode_valid=true`. Invalid episodes remain retained but are excluded;
there are no replacement runs.

## Outcome domains

Efficiency primary: total waiting, mean waiting, episode duration. Secondary:
mean speed and completion/throughput.

Service-distribution primary: maximum vehicle waiting, P95 vehicle waiting,
maximum approach mean waiting and approach waiting range. Secondary: waiting
sample SD, repeated eligible-but-not-selected count and longest non-service
sequence.

Safety: collisions, safety interventions and grant timeouts.

## Frozen descriptive classification

At least two valid matched seeds are required; otherwise `INCONCLUSIVE`.
Gemini-minus-deterministic deltas are used, and lower is better.

`consistent matched-seed improvement` means at least two of three valid
matched seeds meet the relevant per-seed rule. A service seed improves when at
least three of four primary service metrics decrease by at least 1.0 s and
none increases by 1.0 s or more. An efficiency seed improves when at least two
of total waiting, mean waiting and duration decrease by 5.0 s, 0.5 s and 1.0 s
respectively, without material degradation.

`material degradation` means any per-seed increase of at least 10.0 s total
waiting, 1.0 s mean waiting, or 2.0 s episode duration. It is a consistent
efficiency degradation when present in at least two matched seeds.

- `MULTI_DOMAIN_BENEFIT`: consistent service improvement without consistent
  material efficiency degradation (including improvement in both domains).
- `SERVICE_DISTRIBUTION_EFFICIENCY_TRADEOFF`: consistent service improvement
  with consistent material efficiency degradation.
- `EFFICIENCY_ONLY_BENEFIT`: consistent efficiency improvement without the
  service rule.
- `NO_OBSERVED_LLM_BENEFIT`: no consistent matched-seed benefit.
- `DETERMINISTIC_BETTER`: deterministic control meets the symmetric service or
  efficiency rule in at least two matched seeds and Gemini has no qualifying benefit.
- `INCONCLUSIVE`: fewer than two valid matched pairs.

## Claim and stopping boundary

Allowed wording is restricted to this preregistered stress condition and
matched seeds. The experiment cannot establish general Gemini superiority,
fairness optimisation, starvation prevention, real-world safety, or broad
intersection improvement. Any service benefit must report its efficiency cost.

After Stage 1 failure, or after any Stage 2 classification, supplementary
experiment creation stops. No result-driven scenario search is permitted.
